Research publication status (7 November 2026). Three research notes from my earlier collaboration with Professor Jürgen Jost and Dr. Marzieh Eidi have been withdrawn from this blog following Professor Jost's request concerning the disclosure of unfinished joint research. I take that scientific concern seriously. I was nonetheless disappointed that the request included personal assessments of my mathematical and professional development, which I consider inappropriate to the question of publication consent. Original drafts remain preserved in the public source repository as an archival record, but are not published as blog articles.

Circular Sorting: A Small Computational Note

Some explicit permutations for n = 36, 40 and 42, with finite Lean certificates, reproducible checks, and a note on their limitations.

A Quotient-First View of Symmetry-Invariant Distances

Categorical G-spaces, quotient universal properties, and symmetry-invariant molecular distances.
