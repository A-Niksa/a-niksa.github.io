This research note has been withdrawn

Three research notes from my earlier collaboration with Professor Jürgen Jost and Dr. Marzieh Eidi have been withdrawn from this blog following Professor Jost's request concerning the disclosure of unfinished joint research. I take that scientific concern seriously. I was nonetheless disappointed that the request included personal assessments of my mathematical and professional development, which I consider inappropriate to the question of publication consent. Original drafts remain preserved in the public source repository as an archival record, but are not published as blog articles.
