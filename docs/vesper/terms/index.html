Terms of use

The purpose, authorized use, and operating limits of Vesper’s personal briefing setup.

Effective date: 24 October 2026

A personal project

Vesper is a private briefing tool built and operated by Arsha Niksa for personal use. These pages describe that setup; they do not offer a public hosted service, public registration, a subscription, or paid support.

Authorized use

Connect only accounts and sources you own or have permission to access. Google access requires the account holder’s OAuth consent. The Telegram bot is paired to the owner’s private chat. Visiting these information pages does not grant access to the bot or to another person’s data.

The privacy policy explains the data used to provide briefings and how to revoke access or remove stored copies.

Briefing limits

Briefings are summaries and may be incomplete, delayed, or based on cached information. Apple data can only reflect the latest successful sync. Email snippets may omit important context; keyword-based alerts are not independently verified incidents. Tentative ideas are suggestions, not commitments.

The service is scheduled for 7:00 AM Asia/Tehran, but network outages, provider failures, expired authorization, and maintenance can delay or prevent delivery. Check the original calendar, email, task list, or note before acting. Vesper is not an emergency or safety-critical notification service.

Availability and changes

Vesper is an experimental personal tool, provided as available without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation, accuracy, or fitness for a particular purpose. Features may change or stop. Google, Telegram, GitHub, and infrastructure providers operate under their own terms and policies. These project terms do not limit rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
